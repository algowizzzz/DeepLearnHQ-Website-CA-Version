What is the best AI for a small business?

(The #1 question.) Pick one general chatbot and get your team fluent before adding anything else. The value isn't the subscription — it's knowing which tasks to hand it. Our full playbook is ChatGPT for business; here's how the picks break down by job.

📣 Best for marketing & content — ChatGPT / Gemini

Captions, emails, ad variations, and content calendars in minutes — a real force-multiplier for lean teams. Keep a human on strategy and brand voice. Campaign prompts: ChatGPT for marketing.

✍️ Best for writing & comms — Claude

Proposals, docs, and polished client communication — Claude's writing craft and long-document handling make it the pick for anything longer-form. See Claude vs ChatGPT.

📊 Best for admin & spreadsheets — ChatGPT

Draft SOPs, summarise meetings, and — a huge small-business win — write the spreadsheet formula you need in plain English. See ChatGPT for Excel.
