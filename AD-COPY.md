## Compliance check on this set

Verified against AD-POLICY-CHECKLIST.md:

- ✅ No `$4,999`, no "50% off", no Udemy price comparison, no invented value stacks
- ✅ No income claims or implications
- ✅ **No second-person negative attribution.** Ad 1 says "most people", not "you're stuck" — Meta reads the second-person form as implying a personal characteristic, and it is the most common rejection cause for education ads
- ✅ No countdown or urgency in the ads. The 72-hour code is real but per-user, so it belongs on the page after signup, not in an ad where it can't be verified
- ✅ Price, product name and promise identical to the landing page
- ⚠️ **Ad 2 cannot run until the Udemy screenshots verify `{STUDENTS}`.** Ads 1 and 3 carry no proof number and can launch first
