Can ChatGPT Make Videos in 2026? What's Actually Possible

Saad Ahmed·Jul 27, 2026·4 min read

Short answer: ChatGPT itself doesn't render finished videos — but it's a superb video-workflow partner (scripts, storyboards, shot lists), and dedicated AI video tools (like OpenAI's Sora) do generate video from text. So "can ChatGPT make videos?" is really two questions. Here's the honest answer to both.

Can ChatGPT directly make a video?

Not a finished, rendered video on its own — its core output is text (and images via its image features). What it's excellent at is everything around the video: the idea, the script, the hook, the storyboard, the shot list, captions, and the description.

What actually makes AI video

Dedicated text-to-video models do the generation — most notably OpenAI's Sora, plus others in the fast-moving AI-video space. You describe a scene and they produce footage. Quality and length limits change quickly, so check each tool's current capabilities.

The realistic workflow (how people actually do it)

  1. ChatGPT — brainstorm the concept, write the script, and plan the shots.
  2. An AI video tool (Sora or similar) — generate the clips.
  3. An editor — assemble, add music/captions (ChatGPT can draft those too).
