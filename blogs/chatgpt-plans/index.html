The plans, in plain English

  • Free ($0): genuinely capable — everyday writing, explanations, and most study/work tasks. Limits on usage and access to the very newest models.
  • Go (low-cost): a light step up — higher limits than Free without the full Plus price. Good if Free almost covers you.
  • Plus (the standard paid tier, ~$20/mo): more usage, the latest models, and features like advanced data/file tools and projects. The right pick for regular, serious users.
  • Pro (power tier, higher price): maximum usage and the most capable models — for professionals who use it heavily all day.

(Tiers and prices shift regularly — always confirm on OpenAI's official pricing page.)

Which should you choose?

  • Casual / student: Free. It covers explaining, studying, and writing help. See the best AI for students.
  • Regular professional: Plus — worth it once you use it daily and hit Free's limits.
  • Small business / heavy user: Plus per person, or Pro for the heaviest workloads. See the best AI for business.
